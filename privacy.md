# 5. Recall and AI Processing

Recall is Hireytics' intelligence layer that helps users reason across information already stored within their organization's hiring environment.

Depending on the question and available data, Recall may process information such as:

* Job descriptions;
* Candidate profiles;
* Resumes;
* Assessments;
* Interview transcripts;
* Interview feedback;
* Pipeline information; and
* Other hiring information available to the user's organization.

Recall is designed to provide answers based on the information available to it. AI-generated responses may contain errors or incomplete information and should be reviewed by an appropriate human decision-maker.

Hireytics does not intend Recall to independently make employment decisions on behalf of an organization.

Organizations remain responsible for employment decisions, candidate evaluation, compliance, and the appropriate use of information produced by Hireytics.
